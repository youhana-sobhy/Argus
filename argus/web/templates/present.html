The challenge

Network changes are slow and risky today.

The knowledge needed to make a safe change is spread across controllers, command lines and people's heads.

Hard to see

Engineers spend time hunting through screens to answer simple questions about what is configured and what is broken.

Risky to change

A single wrong change can affect live services. Checks depend on who is doing the work that day.

Slow to approve

Reviews happen over email and chat, with no single record of who checked what and when.

What Argus does

Three things, done well.

One platform that sees the network, explains it, and changes it under control.

01 · SEE

Ingest

Collects the full configuration and live state of the fabric into one clear model.

  • Every switch, link, tenant and network
  • Physical and logical topology maps
  • Export to standard formats at any time
02 · UNDERSTAND

Ask

Engineers chat with the network in plain English and get exact answers.

  • "Which networks are unused?"
  • "Are any links down?"
  • Sensitive data is masked before AI sees it
03 · CHANGE

Act

Changes follow one governed path, from request to verified result.

  • Automatic checks before anything runs
  • A second person must approve
  • Verified after, rolled back if needed

AI assistant

An expert that explains, but never acts alone.

Answers from real data

It only answers from the collected network inventory, with exact object names.

Private by design

IP addresses, serial numbers and secrets are masked before anything is sent to an AI model.

Drafts only

The AI can suggest a change, but it always goes through checks and human approval.

Flexible provider

Works offline, with Claude, or with Azure OpenAI. Switching is a single setting.

Built-in safety

Guardrails that never switch off.

Four-eyes rule

Nobody can approve their own change, not even an admin.

Blocks unsafe changes

For example, deleting a network that is still in use is stopped at dry-run.

Always a way back

Every change stores the exact steps to restore the previous state.

Full audit trail

Who did what, when and with which result, ready for compliance.
