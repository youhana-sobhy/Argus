How AutoHero works

Three capabilities, one vendor-neutral model
01 · INGEST

Collect & normalize

Pulls full config and state from the APIC and maps it to one vendor-neutral model. Export as normalized JSON or native APIC JSON.

02 · UNDERSTAND

Chat with your network

Ask about tenants, bridge domains, EPGs, subnets and fabric health. Sensitive values are masked before any AI call.

03 · CHANGE

Plan, approve, execute

Create application profiles and bridge domains, or delete objects, with pre-checks, a diff and four-eyes approval.

04 · ASSURE

Verify & roll back

Post-checks confirm the result on a fresh snapshot. A ready rollback restores the exact previous state.
